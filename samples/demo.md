# Northwind Coffee — Q3 Review

Prepared for the leadership team · 12 September 2026

## Summary

Revenue grew **14 per cent** year on year, driven almost entirely by the
subscription channel. Retail was flat. The northern region outperformed
expectations for the third consecutive quarter, while the southern region
missed target by a small margin.

> The subscription business is now large enough that we should plan for it
> separately rather than treating it as a retail add-on.

## Revenue by channel

| Channel | Q3 2026 | Q3 2025 | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subscription | £2.41m | £1.78m | +35% |
| Retail | £1.92m | £1.90m | +1% |
| Wholesale | £0.64m | £0.71m | −10% |
| **Total** | **£4.97m** | **£4.39m** | **+14%** |

## Regional performance

- **North** — 118% of target. Two new accounts opened in Leeds and Newcastle.
- **Midlands** — 102% of target. Steady, no notable movement.
- **South** — 94% of target. One account lost to a competitor on price.

## Costs

Green coffee prices rose 8 per cent over the quarter. We absorbed this rather
than passing it on, which cost roughly £140,000 in margin. Freight costs fell
slightly, partially offsetting it.

## Risks

1. **Supply chain** — our single Colombian supplier represents 41% of volume.
   A second source has been identified but not yet contracted.
2. **Subscription churn** — currently 4.2% monthly. Acceptable, but it has
   risen from 3.6% and the trend is worth watching.
3. **Currency** — sterling weakness increases the cost of all green coffee.

## Recommendation

Contract the second Colombian supplier before the end of Q4, and commission a
proper churn analysis before setting next year's subscription targets.
